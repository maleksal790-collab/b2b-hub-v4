The Problem

Saudi Arabia’s industrial sector (oil & gas, petrochemicals, construction, manufacturing) runs on highly fragmented procurement. Large buyers struggle with compliance, visibility, and speed. Vendors (especially mid-tier and SMBs) face high friction accessing consistent demand.

The Solution: Dual-Mode Procurement OS

B2B Hub v4 combines two modes in one governed platform:

Storefront Marketplace
Self-serve buying for SMBs with instant checkout and tiered pricing.
Enterprise RFQ Hub
Structured workflows, private buyer lists, negotiated margins, and full audit trails.
Mixed-mode logic automatically switches high-volume or strategic items from storefront to RFQ based on configurable rules.
